## The idea

Check that everything works before the first real project: the schema, the
template, the card on the home page, the detail page and, later, the demo.

## What it does

Nothing at all, on purpose. This entry exists so that every element of the
page — title, summary, image, stack, links — has a concrete example while
the site is being built.

## Under the hood

A project is a folder with a French text (which also carries the shared
data), an English text and a cover image. The build validates everything
and rejects anything incomplete.

## Known limits

It will stay a draft forever: it will never appear on the published site.
